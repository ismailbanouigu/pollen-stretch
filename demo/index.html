Why pollination keeps ecosystems connected

Pollination moves pollen between reproductive parts of flowers. This exchange makes fertilisation and seed production possible. The process can be carried out by insects, birds, bats, wind, or water.

This small biological exchange supports food webs far beyond the flower itself. It helps plants reproduce, gives animals reliable sources of food, and preserves genetic diversity across habitats.
